# Skill Delta To Merge - ch001_loop_01

This file is a PR-style proposal for external review. It does not modify the main Renderer Skill.

## Delta 1：Functional Aliveness Over Decorative Reaction

Observed in Loop:
The best candidates made the protagonist feel alive through useful decisions: verifying abnormality, preserving evidence, setting priorities, testing task logic, and choosing the next action.

Generic Skill Candidate:
When a character faces an absurd mismatch, show aliveness through functional reaction. Prefer judgment, priority, and changed action over gestures, embarrassment, or extra quips.

Scope:
suggested_generic_skill_delta

Why Generic / Why Not:
This applies across genres whenever a capable character meets pressure or abnormal rules. It does not depend on this case's characters or setting.

Risk:
May over-bias all protagonists toward calm competence and reduce valid emotional or impulsive voices.

Regression Test Idea:
Use a seed with an absurd task. Check whether the protagonist makes a concrete judgment or plan. Also check that added human flavor is not just gestures or modern jokes.

## Delta 2：One Resource Proof Is Better Than Inventory

Observed in Loop:
Candidates improved when one concrete late resource carried the contrast. Candidates weakened when they explained too many reward items or turned the resource scene into a catalogue.

Generic Skill Candidate:
For a late, obsolete, or mismatched resource, select the sharpest one or two proof objects. Show why it once mattered, why it is mismatched now, and how it affects the current plan.

Scope:
suggested_generic_skill_delta

Why Generic / Why Not:
This applies to rewards, inheritances, tools, privileges, status, and tasks across genres. The exact item remains case-specific.

Risk:
May cause every object to receive a symbolic memory, slowing the prose.

Regression Test Idea:
Give a seed with multiple rewards. The draft should not explain every reward. At least one resource should change the character's plan.

## Delta 3：Old Conflict Must Be Light But Not Weightless

Observed in Loop:
The strongest drafts kept the old conflict's past pressure while refusing to inflate the old opponent into a major current villain.

Generic Skill Candidate:
When an old conflict is reactivated, show its past weight and current mismatch. Do not make the old opponent heavier than the current plot warrants.

Scope:
suggested_generic_skill_delta

Why Generic / Why Not:
This is generic for old rivals, old debts, old failures, outdated tasks, and delayed revenge structures.

Risk:
Could encourage unnecessary backstory if applied too broadly.

Regression Test Idea:
Check whether the old conflict changes the current action. If not, cut or compress. If the old conflict overwhelms current stakes, mark as over-weighted.

## Delta 4：Absurd Task Needs Executable Hook

Observed in Loop:
The best endings did not stop at "this is absurd." They gave the protagonist a concrete first attempt and an implied fallback.

Generic Skill Candidate:
An absurd or impossible task should become a next-action problem. The ending should leave a target, a first method, and a blocked reward or route.

Scope:
suggested_generic_skill_delta

Why Generic / Why Not:
This applies to system tasks, orders, tests, contracts, prophecies, and rules. The concrete execution method must stay case-specific.

Risk:
May turn chapters into procedural problem-solving too early.

Regression Test Idea:
For an absurd-task seed, check whether the chapter ends with action direction rather than only a joke or explanation.

## Delta 5：Resource Can Reveal Rule Perspective

Observed in Loop:
The final direction worked when the late resource proved what version of reality the rule-carrier was recognizing.

Generic Skill Candidate:
When a rule-carrier is out of sync with reality, a mismatched resource can reveal its perspective. Use this to guide the protagonist's next test.

Scope:
suggested_generic_skill_delta

Why Generic / Why Not:
This is generic for delayed systems, outdated institutions, stale contracts, old permissions, inherited tools, and obsolete tests.

Risk:
May become over-analytical if the prose says "evidence," "data," or "rule perspective" instead of dramatizing it.

Regression Test Idea:
Check that the resource changes the character's plan through action, not through abstract explanation.

## Delta 6：Side Character Gets One Structural Job

Observed in Loop:
Drafts improved when the side character stopped asking serial explanatory questions and instead served one function: public pressure, useful misread, constraint, or reaction.

Generic Skill Candidate:
Side characters in high-concept openings should have one structural job. They should not become a device for explaining the premise.

Scope:
suggested_generic_skill_delta

Why Generic / Why Not:
This applies broadly to witnesses, elders, assistants, officials, rivals, and bystanders.

Risk:
May flatten legitimate ensemble scenes if applied too rigidly.

Regression Test Idea:
Audit side-character dialogue. If most lines only let the protagonist explain known information, reduce or repurpose the character.

## Delta 7：Avoid Engineering Vocabulary In Prose

Observed in Loop:
Round 2 candidate_05 weakened when analytical terms made the scene feel like a design note instead of fiction.

Generic Skill Candidate:
Translate analysis into story action. Avoid terms like anchor, workflow, mechanism, rule boundary, and proof layer unless the world itself would naturally use them.

Scope:
suggested_generic_skill_delta

Why Generic / Why Not:
This is a general prose-quality rule and not tied to this case.

Risk:
Could over-correct and remove useful in-world technical language from sci-fi, game, or system-heavy genres.

Regression Test Idea:
Scan drafts for analytical vocabulary. Keep it only if the POV, genre, or world would naturally use it.

## Delta 8：Case Execution Objects Stay In Experiment

Observed in Loop:
Specific execution objects made this case work, but they are inseparable from this chapter's seed and should not enter generic Skill.

Generic Skill Candidate:
Do not merge specific execution objects. Merge only the abstraction: use existing, rule-recognizable elements from the seed to test the task.

Scope:
case_specific_only

Why Generic / Why Not:
The abstraction is generic; the concrete objects are case-specific.

Risk:
If merged directly, future unrelated chapters may mechanically reuse inappropriate objects or rituals.

Regression Test Idea:
Before merging any delta, scan for private names, concrete objects, locations, and task text from the case. If present, reject or abstract.

## Delta 9：Do Not Add New Props To Sharpen A Hook

Observed in Loop:
Round 2 candidate_06 added a new prop that made the hook more fun but risked leaving the seed.

Generic Skill Candidate:
When converging, do not add new props, records, institutions, tools, or helpers solely to sharpen a hook. Reuse what the seed or draft already provides.

Scope:
suggested_generic_skill_delta

Why Generic / Why Not:
This is a general anti-drift rule for revision passes.

Risk:
May prevent useful invention in early creative exploration if applied before the convergence phase.

Regression Test Idea:
During late-round revisions, list all newly introduced objects. If a new object is not in the seed and only exists to make the hook clever, remove it.

## Delta 10：Rejected: More Humor Equals More Aliveness

Observed in Loop:
Light humor helped only when it came from mismatch and action. Humor that existed only as cleverness risked weakening pressure.

Generic Skill Candidate:
Do not treat more jokes, modern phrasing, or witty replies as the route to character aliveness.

Scope:
rejected_delta

Why Generic / Why Not:
This should not be a positive writing rule. It is a rejected shortcut. Humor must be subordinate to character judgment and scene pressure.

Risk:
If treated as a positive rule, future drafts will drift into quip-driven prose and lose world tone.

Regression Test Idea:
Remove each humorous line. If nothing about action, judgment, or mismatch changes, the line is likely decorative and should be cut.
